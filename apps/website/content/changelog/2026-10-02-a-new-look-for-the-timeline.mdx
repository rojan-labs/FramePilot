### Improved

- **A calmer, clearer timeline.** The timeline and its toolbar have a new neutral design, in
  both dark and light themes. Each kind of clip has its own colour: teal video, blue audio,
  orange text, a darker orange for captions and purple effects. You can tell your layers apart
  at a glance.
- **More picture, less chrome.** A clip's frames or waveform fill the whole clip under a thin
  name bar.
- **Easy to see what's selected.** The selected clip has a white ring (black in light theme),
  and the playhead is a thin line with a shield-shaped head.
- **Track controls you can find.** Hide, mute, lock and solo now show on every track, dimmed
  until you use them.
- **A menu button on every track.** The new **⋯** button opens the track menu. Before, it only
  opened with a right-click. It works from the keyboard too.
- **Speed at a glance.** Sped-up, reversed, frozen and ramped clips show a badge such as `2×`.
- **Markers and transitions are easier to read.** A marker is a small flag on the ruler, so it
  is no longer mistaken for the playhead. A transition shows a small bow-tie on the cut.
- **Nothing to relearn.** Every shortcut, drag, trim and menu works exactly as before.
