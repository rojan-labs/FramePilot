### New

- **AI emphasis finds the words that carry the idea.** Your selected AI model weighs meaning,
  emotion, pauses, delivery and sentence structure, then gives a small number of anchor words the
  visual hierarchy they deserve. You can replace every suggestion, and captions still work with a
  clearly labelled local fallback when the AI is unavailable.

- **Ask the editing agent to design captions end to end.** It can now choose from the real font and
  template library, emphasize only words that are actually spoken, and control placement, size,
  rotation, width, alignment, spacing, backgrounds and animation. Every change remains reviewable,
  editable and undoable just like a manual caption adjustment.

- **Twenty-two creative fonts, ready everywhere.** Pick from familiar sans, bold display,
  editorial serif, monospace and handwritten families. They are included with FramePilot, so the
  same typography appears in preview and export on another machine.

- **Captions are editable objects in the Program monitor.** Drag one into place, resize its wrap
  width, rotate it, or double-click to rewrite it. Alignment, line height and safe-area behavior
  carry through to export.

- **Six new production looks.** Semantic Anchor, Editorial Contrast, Compact Tier, Kinetic Stack,
  Handwritten Zone and Social Headline bring creator, podcast, editorial, lyric and short-form
  personalities—not just different fonts.

### Improved

- **Segmentation now composes around emphasized words.** Cue and line breaks still follow pauses,
  punctuation and reading speed, while reserving enough visual room for the anchor word so phrases
  stay balanced and readable.

- **Generated captions remain a starting point, not a locked result.** Rewrite, split, merge,
  restyle, reposition and undo without returning to transcription.
