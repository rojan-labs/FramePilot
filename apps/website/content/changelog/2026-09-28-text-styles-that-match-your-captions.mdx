### New

- **A Text panel built around styles.** Add a heading, subheading or body text in one click, or
  pick from ready-made styles: bold titles, lower thirds, callouts, subscribe and follow buttons,
  and quotes. Each style is shown in its real font. Click to add it at the playhead, or drag it
  onto the timeline.
- **Every caption look works as a title.** All caption styles are also available as title styles,
  so your on-screen text can match your captions exactly.
- **Restyle in one click.** With a title selected, press **Apply** on any style to restyle it. Its
  words and position stay as they are.
- **Recent styles** stay at the top of the panel, and the search finds styles by name, category or
  font.

### Improved

- **Titles use the caption fonts.** Titles now offer the same 92 fonts as captions, plus outline,
  shadow, letter spacing, line height, case, italic, see-through letters and a rounded background.
- **What you see is what you export.** Titles are drawn by the same engine as captions, so the
  export matches the preview. Titles no longer switch to a different font on another computer.
