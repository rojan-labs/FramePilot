### New

- **The site opens with a ripple delete.** The editors you already know appear on a track, a
  playhead sweeps through, and every one of them lands in the bin in the corner. FramePilot is
  the clip that stays and becomes the logo. It plays once, you can skip it by scrolling, and it
  stays still if your system asks for reduced motion.
- **Every page is on the same timeline.** Pricing is one clip with one price, downloads are
  three tracks with your platform highlighted, and the docs, blog, and changelog sit on the same
  ruler. The bin stays in the corner of the home page, with the retired tools still in it.
