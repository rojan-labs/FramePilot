### New

- **Find and remove silence, automatically.** Ask the agent to clean up your
  footage and it now pinpoints every silent gap for you, the long pauses, the
  dead air between takes, the "um, let me start over" moments, so you can trim
  them in one pass instead of hunting frame by frame.
- **Spot every scene change.** FramePilot can now detect where each new shot
  begins, making it effortless to split a long recording into clips, drop in
  b-roll at the right beats, or jump straight to the moment you care about.

### Improved

- **Faster, steadier imports.** Large clips and long recordings now load more
  reliably, and FramePilot no longer gets stuck waiting on a file that's slow to
  read, you get a clear result instead of a spinner that never ends.
