### New

- **A Text panel built around styles.** Add a heading, subheading or body text in one click, or
  pick from 60 ready-made styles: bold text overlays, lower thirds, callouts, subscribe and follow
  buttons, quotes, handwritten scripts and retro looks. Each style is shown in its real font.
  Click to add it at the playhead, or drag it onto the timeline.
- **Every caption font, for your text overlays too.** The new Fonts tab lists all 92 caption fonts,
  each shown in its own face. Click one to set your text overlay in it.
- **Restyle in one click.** With a text overlay selected, press **Apply** on any style to restyle it. Its
  words and position stay as they are.
- **Recent styles** stay at the top of the panel, and the search finds styles by name, category or
  font.
- **One bounding box for everything on the preview.** Click a clip, a photo, a sticker, a shape
  or some text, and drag to move it. Drag a corner to resize it (hold Shift to stretch it), or
  drag the round handle to rotate it. It works at any zoom, never flips inside out, and one undo
  takes back each drag. On text, a side handle rewraps the words and a double-click lets you type.
- **Frosted-glass text.** Text can sit on a frosted background that blurs the video behind it,
  in the preview and in the export.
- **Stretch and squash.** Anything on the preview can now be stretched wider or taller, not just
  zoomed. Hold Shift while resizing, or use Stretch X and Stretch Y in the Inspector.

### Improved

- **Text overlays use the caption fonts.** Text overlays now offer the same 92 fonts as captions, plus outline,
  shadow, letter spacing, line height, case, italic, see-through letters and a rounded background.
- **What you see is what you export.** Text overlays are drawn by the same engine as captions, so the
  export matches the preview. Text overlays no longer switch to a different font on another computer.
