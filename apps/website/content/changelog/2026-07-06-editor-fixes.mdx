### Fixed

- **Click a text overlay to edit it.** Selecting a text overlay right on the
  preview now works as you'd expect, click to pick it, drag to move it, pull a
  handle to resize it, and double-click to change the words. No more clicks
  slipping through to the clip behind it.
- **The shape menu opens where you expect.** Choosing your canvas shape,
  Landscape, Reels, Square and the rest, now opens fully in view instead of
  tucking behind the video or running off the edge of the window, and it lines
  up neatly with the playback buttons.
- **Tooltips stay on screen.** Longer tooltips no longer spill off the side of
  the window, they wrap and stay fully visible.
