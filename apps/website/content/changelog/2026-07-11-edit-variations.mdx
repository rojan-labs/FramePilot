### New

- **Show 2 alternatives (Edit mode).** When you ask the AI for a quick edit, a
  new "Show 2 alternatives" toggle lets it propose two independent takes
  instead of one. Flip between "Take A" and "Take B" in the review card —
  same before/after player, re-pointed at whichever take you're looking at —
  and accept the one you like. The other is discarded, never left half-applied.
- **Honest about cost.** A second take is a second real AI call, so it's
  off by default and the toggle says so up front. When you turn it on, the
  usage chip reflects the combined cost of both takes, not just one.
- **Only where it makes sense.** Deterministic one-click actions (remove
  silences, add captions, fix pacing, and the rest) never show this toggle —
  running the exact same recipe twice would just give you the identical
  result, so there's nothing to compare.
