### New

- **Cross-project memory.** A new **Settings → Memory** panel lets you set your
  editing defaults once — who your videos are for, your brand and caption style,
  how tight you like the cut, and where you export — and every new project
  inherits them. A project's own choices always win, so nothing is ever
  overridden without your say-so. Your defaults follow you, not the file.
- **Save a run as a recipe.** Save an edit you like as a named workflow and it
  becomes a one-word shortcut: type its name later and it replays instantly,
  with **no AI call at all**. The more you teach FramePilot, the faster and
  cheaper your everyday edits get. Manage your saved workflows in Settings →
  Memory.

### Improved

- **Sturdier agent runs.** When something goes wrong mid-run — a slow model, a
  step that can't complete — the agent now recovers on its own: it retries,
  falls back to an instant recipe or a cheaper model, works around the failed
  step, or pauses honestly for your review. It never pretends a step succeeded.
- **Instant commands stay instant, even as FramePilot grows.** Common edits like
  "remove the silences", "add captions", and "export for Reels" run with no AI
  call, and the groundwork is now in place to keep them fast and cheap no matter
  how many new capabilities we add.
