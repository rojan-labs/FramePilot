### Improved

- **Effects now appear in the preview you use by default.** Looks and stylised effects update
  directly in the program monitor, so you can judge the edit before exporting it.

- **Every monitor action stays within reach.** Orientation, before-and-after compare, loop,
  composition grid, safe areas, fit and zoom, fullscreen, frame stepping, seeking, and
  timecode stay available throughout editing.

- **One dependable preview, with no engine switch to manage.** The older preview option and
  its Settings control are gone, so effects and compositing always use the same monitor.

### Fixed

- **Portrait and custom canvases keep their true shape.** The picture now fits cleanly inside
  both the available width and height, without stretching or losing its orientation when
  panels move or the project switches between landscape and portrait.

- **The progress bar now sits where it belongs.** It spans the monitor above the centered
  playback controls instead of being squeezed against the right edge.

- **A broken source no longer snaps your edit back to the start.** Its error stays visible
  while timeline timing, captions, effects, and history remain usable.
