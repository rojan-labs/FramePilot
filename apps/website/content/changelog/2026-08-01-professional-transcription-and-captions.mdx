### New

- **TwelveLabs is now a transcription provider.** Choose it in Settings and reuse
  the same key as Media intelligence. FramePilot indexes only the clip you chose,
  then brings its native word timing back into the editor.

### Improved

- **Local transcription uses a substantially stronger multilingual model.** Word
  timing follows the spoken audio — including silence before the first word — so
  generated captions land where the voice actually begins.

- **Caption styles are quicker to compare.** Browse the complete catalog with
  **All**, narrow it by category, or search by a style's name or behavior. Richer
  cards preview the real caption treatment when you hover or focus them.

- **Waiting feels dependable.** Fast cached transcripts do not flash a loader;
  longer jobs show honest provider-specific status and elapsed time without a fake
  percentage.

### Fixed

- **A TwelveLabs key no longer changes providers behind your back.** The provider
  you choose is the provider FramePilot uses. If indexing or transcription fails,
  the existing transcript stays intact and the editor explains what happened.
