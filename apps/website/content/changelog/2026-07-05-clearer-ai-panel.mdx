### Improved

- **Watch the assistant think.** The AI panel now streams the assistant's real
  reasoning as it works, so you can follow what it's doing instead of staring at
  generic placeholder text.
- **Calmer, more honest progress.** We removed the misleading percentages and
  "100%" markers. AI work doesn't really have a percentage. Now you see a subtle
  loading indicator while the assistant works, and the panel simply goes quiet
  when it's done.
- **Know exactly what each step did.** Every action shows an at-a-glance status,
  working, done, needs attention, or failed, with a short summary and a tooltip.
  Open "View details" to see the full picture, copy anything with one click, and
  if a step fails, hover to see why.
- **A smoother place to type.** The message box now grows to fit longer, multi-line
  messages while the add and send buttons stay right where you expect them, plus
  a clearer stop button for when you want to halt the assistant mid-thought.

### Fixed

- **AI edits actually go through now.** The assistant sometimes rejected its own
  changes, like adding or trimming a clip, and finished with "no changes" even
  though it had real work to show. Those edits now apply reliably, so asking the
  AI to build or adjust your timeline produces an edit you can review.
