### New

- **Resize and reposition clips right in the preview.** Click a clip in the
  monitor to get a selection box with corner handles — drag to move, pull a
  corner to scale. Every gesture is a single undoable edit.
- **Canvas presets.** Switch your project between 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, and
  21:9 from the monitor — the preview, guides, and export all follow instantly.
- **GitHub Models and GitHub Copilot.** Bring your GitHub token and run the
  editing agent on GitHub-hosted models, straight from Settings → AI.

### Improved

- **Previews now play lightweight proxies.** Imported footage gets a fast
  preview copy automatically, so scrubbing and cuts stay smooth even with heavy
  camera files — exports still use your originals at full quality.
- **The assistant streams its thinking instantly.** Reasoning appears token by
  token with proper formatting — no more buffered paragraphs or shimmer
  placeholders — and long sessions stay fast from the first message to the
  thousandth.
- **A quieter, quicker workspace.** The AI panel opens first, tool details fold
  away until you want them, the app launches straight into one composed frame,
  and zooming a long timeline stays smooth with thumbnails that never vanish.

### Fixed

- Stopping the assistant now always stops it — even if you switched models
  mid-run.
- Cutting from video to a still image no longer flashes, and gaps in the
  timeline show the empty state instead of a stale frame.
