### New

- **Add text by dragging.** Grab **Text** from the Overlays panel and drop it
  anywhere on the timeline to create an overlay right where you want it.
- **Edit right on the video.** Select a text overlay and work with it directly in
  the preview, drag to move it, pull a side handle to set how wide it wraps, and
  double-click to type. No fiddly forms.
- **Style it your way.** A new Text panel puts everything in one place: content,
  colour, font and weight, size, alignment, a background, on-screen position, and
  in/out animations with timing. The preview shows exactly how it will look and
  moves, even as you scrub.
- **Pick your transitions.** Clicking the **+** between two clips now opens a
  quick menu of every transition (fade, cross-dissolve, push, zoom, blur)
  instead of just dropping in a default.
- **A composition grid.** Turn on a rule-of-thirds grid in the monitor to line up
  your shots.

### Improved

- **A calmer preview.** The area around your video now matches the app instead of
  harsh black, the frame fits perfectly at any window size, and fast cut-heavy
  edits play back more smoothly.
- **Status where you expect it.** "Saved to…" and other messages now appear
  quietly in the header instead of popping up as a separate toast.
- **Snappier zooming.** Zooming the timeline in and out repeatedly no longer bogs
  the app down, thumbnails and waveforms are cached smartly and reused.
- **Leaner AI.** Behind the scenes the assistant now sends less redundant data
  with each step, so it works within tighter model limits.

### Fixed

- **No more empty "proposed edit."** When the assistant only made a plan, replied
  in chat, or hit a snag, it no longer shows an empty edit card with nothing to
  apply, and the "Apply all" count stays accurate.
