### Improved

- **The agent recovers on its own.** If the AI service is briefly busy or your
  connection stumbles, FramePilot quietly retries instead of giving up, so a
  momentary blip no longer interrupts your edit.
- **It remembers the whole conversation.** Ask a follow-up like "now make it
  vertical" and the agent knows exactly what "it" is. Your back-and-forth builds
  on itself instead of starting over each time.

### Fixed

- **No more endless spinners.** If a response ever stalls, FramePilot now tells
  you clearly and offers a one-click retry, rather than leaving you waiting.
