### Coming soon

This is a preview of work in testing. **None of it is in your copy of FramePilot yet** — it
arrives in a later update, once it meets our quality bar on real footage.

- **Remove the background from a shot.** Pick the person (or let FramePilot find them) and they
  are cut out on every frame, so something else can sit behind them. It runs entirely on your
  computer; your footage is never uploaded.
- **Blur a face and have it stay on the face.** Draw a shape around a face or a number plate,
  let FramePilot follow it through the shot, and blur only what is inside.
- **Put a title behind someone.** Once the background is removed, the text slides between the
  person and the scene in one step.
- **Masks you draw yourself.** Rectangles, ellipses, pen paths, freehand shapes, gradients and
  split screens, each animatable, with what you see in the preview matching your export.
- **Ask for it in plain words.** "Blur the faces except the host" or "put the title behind her":
  when it is not sure who you mean, FramePilot shows you the faces and asks, rather than guessing.
  Remembering who is who in a project is off unless you turn it on, and you can delete it any time.
- **You check the hard moments.** Anything the automatic checks were unsure about is listed so
  you can look at it, fix it with a brush, or approve it. Nothing is called "verified" until you
  have looked.
- **Long jobs survive a restart.** If the app quits halfway through, it picks up where it left
  off the next time you open the project.
