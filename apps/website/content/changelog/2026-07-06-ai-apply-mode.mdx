### New

- **Choose how the AI's edits reach your timeline.** A new Apply mode control
  in the assistant sidebar puts you in charge. Keep **Manual** (the default) and
  every edit shows up as a card you review and Accept or Reject, just like today.
  Switch to **Auto** and each edit lands on your timeline the moment the assistant
  proposes it, so a multi-step request can build and adjust your video hands-free
  while you watch. Either way, every edit is checked before it's applied, and a
  single Undo takes it back.

### Fixed

- **No more "busy but nothing happened" runs.** When you ask the assistant for
  something, it now either shows you the edits you can apply or tells you clearly
  that nothing could be applied and why, instead of looking busy and leaving you
  with no result.
- **Long error messages stay tidy.** Lengthy messages no longer spill outside
  their cards in the sidebar, so the panel stays readable.
