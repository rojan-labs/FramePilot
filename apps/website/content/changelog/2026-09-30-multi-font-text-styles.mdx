### New

- **Combos: text styles with more than one font.** Professional titles rarely use a single
  font. They pair a small, widely spaced label with a big headline, or lay a handwritten word
  over bold capitals. The new **Combos** section in the Text panel has nine of these: kicker +
  headline, documentary, script + caps, fashion, serif + wide, wedding, chapter title, creator
  and explainer. They follow the sizes, spacing and font pairings title designers use.
- **More designed styles.** Sport, Shout, Hook chips, End card and Vlog titles join their
  sections.
- **Every line in its own look.** Each line of a multi-font style has its own font, size and
  colour. Type your words as usual and press Shift+Enter to start the next line. Change the size
  in the Inspector, and every line scales together.

### Improved

- **Better lower thirds, stats and quotes.** Name tag, Accent bar, Location, Smoked and News bar
  now show a name with a smaller title under it. Big number puts a label under the number,
  Testimonial adds the speaker's name, and Cinematic and Editorial have a subtitle line.
- **Styles are previewed over real photos.** Each section shows its styles over a photo that
  suits it, instead of a flat grey box, so you can see how a style reads over real video.
- **The assistant knows the new styles.** Ask for a lower third or a chapter title, and it writes
  each part on its own line so every part gets its own font.
- **New lines are kept.** A new line typed on the preview with Shift+Enter now stays when you
  finish typing.
