### New

- **FramePilot is officially available.** Download the app for macOS, Windows,
  and Linux, activate it with your subscription, and hand the tedious parts of
  editing, cuts, captions, silence, pacing, off to an agent that works on a
  real, professional timeline.
- **One simple subscription.** Get the whole editor for one price, the AI
  agent, the render engine, and every new tool we ship while you're a member.
  No ads, no watermarks, and no per-export fees. Choose monthly or save with
  annual billing, and cancel anytime.
- **Your work stays yours.** Editing happens locally on your machine, every
  change the agent makes is reversible, and every export is checked before it
  finishes, so you're always in control of the final cut.

### Improved

- **Effortless updates.** New versions install automatically in the background,
  so you're always on the latest FramePilot without lifting a finger.
