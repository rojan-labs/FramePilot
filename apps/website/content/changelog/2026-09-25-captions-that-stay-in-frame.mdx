### Improved

- **Captions stay where you put them.** A caption with a big shadow, a large accent word or a
  large font could be drawn off to the right and cut off at the edge of the video. It now sits
  exactly where its position says.
- **What you see is what you export.** The editor's preview now wraps captions onto the same
  number of lines as the exported video, and places top and bottom captions at the same height.
- **"No more than two lines" is checkable.** Ask for a line limit and the assistant can now count
  the lines of every caption in the video, and spot any caption too wide for the frame, instead of
  checking one or two by eye.
- **Highlighted words stay highlighted.** Changing the caption font or colour no longer quietly
  removes the words you had emphasised.
- **No more false warnings about out-of-date captions** when a second caption track shows the same
  words.
- **A clearer finish.** When the assistant checks its last edit after replying, you now see
  "Checking the edit…". Press Stop or just send your next message to skip the check. Your request
  still counts as done, and the summary lists your caption changes in one line.
