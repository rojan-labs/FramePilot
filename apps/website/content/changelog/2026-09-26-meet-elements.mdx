### New

- **Stock is now Elements.** The left-rail tab that searched Pexels is now called **Elements** and
  sits second, right after Assets: one shelf for everything you put on the picture that you
  didn't film. **Photos**, **Videos**, **Shapes** and **Stickers** are its sub-tabs, and Photos
  and Videos share one search box, so switching between them searches the same words. What you
  place looks the same on the monitor as in your export. Elements is in the desktop app; the
  browser version doesn't have the Elements tab. Your saved projects, and the photos and videos
  already in them, are untouched.
- **1,595 stickers that work offline.** The whole Fluent Emoji 3D set from Microsoft comes inside
  the app, so nothing downloads when you add one. Browse 251 of them hand-picked into thirteen
  collections (Reactions, Celebrate, Hearts, Money and more), the set's nine groups, your
  **Recent** stickers and your **Favourites** (click the star, or press **F**). Search by name or
  paste the emoji itself. Click a sticker to drop it at the playhead, or drag it onto the
  timeline; a small dot marks the ones already in your project.
- **Stickers you can style and swap.** Move, resize, turn and fade a sticker like any picture.
  The Inspector gives it an **Outline** and a **Shadow** that follow the art itself, and says
  **Enlarged beyond its sharp size** before a sticker would export soft. **Replace…** (or
  **Replace sticker…** on a right-click) swaps it for another and keeps its timing, place and
  animation. If a sticker's file goes missing from a project, it comes back when you open it.
- **Callouts for screen recordings and demos.** The **Shapes** tab opens on the six you reach for
  most: a highlight box, a filled box, an ellipse, a translucent marker, an arrow and an
  underline. Click one to drop it at the playhead, drag it on the monitor to move or resize it,
  or pull an arrow's tip onto whatever it points at. Style its fill, stroke, corners and arrow
  head in the Inspector.
- **A whole library of shapes.** Behind those six are about a hundred more (stars, speech
  bubbles, frames, curved and hand-drawn arrows, rings, numbered badges for your steps) and about
  1,700 icons, with category chips, search and a colour row. Drag a tile onto the timeline to
  place it where you drop it, and swap one shape for another in the Inspector without losing its
  colours or place. Shapes get their own colour on the timeline, so they're easy to spot. A
  project with shapes needs this version of FramePilot or newer: an older one won't open it,
  so your shapes are never quietly dropped.
- **Animate stickers, shapes and titles.** Each can now come in, go out and loop. Pick Fade, Pop,
  one of four slides, Wipe or Blur for the entrance and exit, with a length, and Pulse, Float,
  Wiggle, Bounce, Spin or Blink while it's on screen, with a speed and an amount. A sliding exit
  leaves the way it came in. Find it all in the Inspector's new **Animation** section, or
  right-click a graphic and choose **Animation…**. Every change is one undo. A title's In and Out
  now live here too, instead of on the Text tab.
- **Outlines, glows, shadows and masks on photos, stickers and titles.** A mask on a photo or a
  sticker, an outline, glow or drop shadow, and a track matte on a title now show on the monitor
  and in the export. Before, they were saved but never shown. You can outline or shadow a
  sticker or a photo without drawing a mask first (an outline on a photo becomes a border), and
  a title's outline follows its letters.
- **Categories, orientation and drag for Photos and Videos.** Category chips (Business,
  Technology, People, Nature, City, Abstract, Backgrounds, Food, Travel, Textures) are
  ready-made searches: one click is one search of your Pexels allowance, and clicking the same
  chip again costs nothing. An orientation filter starts on your project's shape, so a vertical
  short is offered vertical shots first. Drag a tile onto the timeline to place it where you
  drop it.
- **Picture-in-picture in one click.** Beside **Add**, which still places a full-frame cutaway
  where there's room, **Add as overlay** lays the photo or video over your footage at the
  playhead: centred, at 40% of its fitted size, above your footage and under your titles,
  stickers and shapes. Move and resize it on the monitor like any clip.
- **Your own logo or screenshot, on top.** Images in your media bin have an **Add as overlay**
  button too (or focus the card and press ⌘/Ctrl + Shift + Enter). A logo, a screenshot or a
  cut-out goes over your footage at the playhead the same way, keeping its see-through edges.
- **Drop stickers and shapes right where you want them.** Drag a sticker or shape tile onto the
  monitor and it lands at the playhead, exactly where you let go. A sticker you drag onto the
  timeline now shows up in **Recent**, just like one you click.
- **Ask the assistant for any of it.** "Add a fire emoji when I say 'this is fire'", "box the
  Export button when I say export", "number each step", "a speech bubble over the host", "make
  the arrow pop in and the sticker pulse", "outline this sticker". It searches the same stickers
  and shapes you see, and looks at the frame to place them. Other AI apps you've connected to
  FramePilot can find, draw, restyle and animate shapes; stickers are added in the FramePilot app
  itself.

### Improved

- **The assistant places elements like a careful editor.** It knows every sticker and shape
  already on your timeline and where each one sits, so "make all the highlight boxes red and
  thicker" restyles the boxes you have, in place, and "remove the stickers" removes only the
  stickers. When it reviews its edit, it points out a sticker over a face, an element under your
  captions or under TikTok's, Reels' or Shorts' own buttons, more than three elements on screen
  at once, a sticker blown up past its sharp size, and a loop that no longer covers its clip,
  while a callout stays on whatever it points at. A request for a sticker or a callout isn't
  finished until one is placed, and it won't put an element entirely outside the frame.

### Fixed

- **New photos, videos and b-roll go under your graphics.** A photo or video added into empty
  time, or a b-roll clip the assistant added, could land on a new layer above everything else
  and hide your titles, stickers and shapes. They now go in front of your footage and under your
  graphics.
- **Stickers stay sharp on vertical and 4K projects.** A new sticker used to be a third of the
  frame high everywhere, which on a vertical short or a 4K export is more than twice the size of
  its art, so it exported soft. It's now as big as it stays sharp there (about a fifth of the
  height). Landscape 1080p projects are unchanged, and a size you set is kept.
- **A rotated title keeps all its letters.** A title turning through a right angle lost most of
  its word, on the monitor and in the export. It now shows in full.
- **Photos and titles fade, crop and animate in the export.** Opacity and its keyframes, fades,
  dissolves, wipes and the other transitions on a photo or a title did nothing in the export or
  on the desktop monitor; now they work. A photo's crop is respected, so an auto-reframed
  landscape photo in a vertical project no longer shows bars. A title's In and Out animation,
  which only played in the browser preview, now plays on the desktop monitor and in the export
  too. A transparent PNG keeps its see-through edges while it fades.
