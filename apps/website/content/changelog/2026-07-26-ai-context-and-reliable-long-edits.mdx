### New

- **Know when a request is getting crowded.** A small ring beside Send shows how much of
  the current AI request's context is in use. Hover or focus it for the used and total
  token counts plus the exact amount remaining.

### Fixed

- **Long AI edits keep moving.** If the fast planning path cannot execute a valid graph,
  FramePilot continues the same request with its general editing agent instead of stopping.
  The selected provider stays consistent throughout, oversized timelines keep coverage
  from beginning to end, and incompatible Ollama sampling settings recover automatically.
