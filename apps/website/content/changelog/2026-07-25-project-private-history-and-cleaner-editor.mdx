### Improved

- **Preview controls stay out of your way.** Playback remains centered below the picture,
  while canvas, guide, zoom, and fullscreen controls sit together at the top right.
- **Panels adapt as you resize them.** Transcript phrases wrap naturally, and the Assets
  header, filters, and import controls reorganize cleanly in a narrow rail.

### Fixed

- **AI chats stay with their project.** Switching projects no longer brings unrelated
  conversation history along with it.
