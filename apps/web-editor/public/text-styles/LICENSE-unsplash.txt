Text panel tile backgrounds
===========================

The photos behind the Text panel's style tiles (one per style category). A text style is judged
against a picture, as it will be used, so each tile draws its sample over real footage-like
photography rather than a flat panel colour.

Every photo is from Unsplash, under the Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license): free to
use for commercial and non-commercial purposes, no permission or attribution required. They are
credited here anyway. Downloaded 2026-09-30 through Lorem Picsum (https://picsum.photos), which
serves Unsplash photos, at 640x360 and re-encoded to WebP (cwebp -q 74).
