## Context

What problem are we solving? What forces are at play (technical, product, team,
timeline)? Link relevant PRD sections (e.g. [`../../PRD.md`](../../PRD.md)) and plan items
([`../../plan/PLAN.md`](../../plan/PLAN.md)). State constraints and assumptions. Keep it
factual — this is the "why" future readers will need.

## Decision

The decision, stated in one or two clear sentences ("We will …"). Then the specifics:
what exactly we are adopting, and any scope/boundaries.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Include:

- Positive consequences.
- Negative consequences / costs / risks accepted.
- Follow-up work or guardrails this decision implies.

## Alternatives Considered

Each realistic alternative, with a one-line reason it was not chosen. Capturing rejected
options is as valuable as the decision itself.
