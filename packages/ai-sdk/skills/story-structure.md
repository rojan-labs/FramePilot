# Story structure

## Purpose

Make the timeline communicate a causal progression before pacing and polish.

## When to use

Long-form, vlogs, essays, interviews, documentaries, or a dragging middle.

## When not to use

Do not restructure at sentence level when cadence/causality would break, or apply a formula against the brief.

## Required inputs

Audience promise, mapped narrative/visual evidence, must-keep beats, target duration, and current sequence.

## Expected outputs

A segment outline, deliberate order, removed redundancy, and labeled chapters where useful.

## Core philosophy

Structure is selection under a promise: each segment must advance stakes, understanding, or payoff.

## Professional heuristics

- Use a truthful cold open when appropriate; compress setup.
- Reorder whole ideas, not isolated sentences.
- Ask whether removing a segment weakens the payoff; if not, cut it.
- Re-hook at genuine section changes, not on a timer.

## Decision framework

Map segments → state function/strength → choose opening/payoff → order escalation → remove redundancy → review causality → add chapter markers.

## Common mistakes

Front-loading every strong moment, breaking thought continuity, keeping expensive-but-irrelevant footage, or polishing before structure.

## Verification checklist

- Opening promise is paid off.
- Each segment changes the viewer's state.
- Causality and speech continuity survive.
- Ending stops after resolution.

## Recovery advice

If reordering confuses context, restore the smallest bridge or move the whole segment back; never wipe and rebuild the track.
